How to package program into an exe.

mvn clean package && New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force target\jpkg-input | Out-Null && Copy-Item target\bnbattlesandbox-1.1.jar target\jpkg-input\ && jpackage --type app-image --name "BattleNationsBattleSandbox" --input target\jpkg-input --main-jar bnbattlesandbox-1.1.jar --main-class hero.roland.bnsim.Main --add-modules java.base,java.desktop,java.logging --jlink-options "--strip-debug --no-man-pages --no-header-files" --app-content packaging\README.txt,LICENSE,THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md,licenses --dest target\dist
